About
Our Tree Tracker
A community-driven platform for monitoring and preserving urban tree cover.
What we do
Field trackers log growth data (height, diameter, health) for registered trees. Sponsors support tree maintenance through monthly contributions. All data is publicly viewable for transparency.
Who it's for
Trackers — authorised field workers who inspect trees and log measurements.
Sponsors — citizens and organisations who fund tree care.
Public — anyone can browse tree profiles and growth history.
Coverage
Currently operational in schools across Tamil Nadu. Each tree is tagged with a unique ID linking it to its location and species.
