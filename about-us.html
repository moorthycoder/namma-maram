About
Namma Maram
Nature and us are one and same.
Our dual objective
We work toward two goals that feed each other: urban tree cover and community leadership. When young people plant and nurture a tree, they build responsibility, patience and teamwork — while the city gains a greener canopy. The tree grows because the leader cares; the leader grows because the tree needs care.
How Namma Maram helps
This app is the specific implementation. Students and field workers register trees under projects like Green TN 2025 and School Green Drive 2024, log growth measurements, and keep each tree healthy through funded care. Sponsors fund the care; everyone sees the results. Every logged encounter is one more act of leadership and one more step toward a healthier urban forest.
Where we work
Across Tamil Nadu, collaborating with schools, colleges, companies and individuals. Our field work spans the state — from the Western Ghats to the Bay of Bengal, and from the Tirupati hills to the Thoothukudi beaches. Every tree carries a unique ID, a species-coloured pin on the map, and a story of someone learning to lead.
